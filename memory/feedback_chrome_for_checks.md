When a check needs a real browser (a signed-in GitHub settings page, a deployed site, a staging read) or a UX audit cannot reach 1440/768/375 px or a theme in the built-in pane, use the Claude in Chrome tools without asking first.

**Why:** the owner allowed it after checks stalled on the built-in pane's limits.

**How to apply:** the safety rules do not change: no credential entry, no purchases, no settings changes, and form submits still need the owner's yes. Other AI tools may lack Chrome: they list what they could not cover under "Not covered" in the report.
